Material

A small vocabulary, deeply refined

Five tokens carry the whole system: glass fill, hairline border, inset bevel, ambient float, and a single charcoal anchor. Every component composes from them.

Layered glass

Three white-alpha tiers — 0.55, 0.75, 0.85 — keep panes legible over the pearl backdrop. Backdrop blur and saturate hold the illusion through scroll and motion.

surface · 3 tiers

Pill geometry

Continuous 9999px radius across buttons, inputs, tabs, chips, and the command dock. Cards relax to 24px so rectangular content still reads cleanly.

radius · pill / xl

Refracted bevels

A top-left highlight, a softer return on the bottom-right, and a quiet hairline border. Together they form the rim that makes each pane feel like real material.

elevation · glass-bevel

Pearl backdrop

A cool #eef1f6 page tone with a soft aurora wash gives the glass something quietly chromatic to refract — without leaking color into the components themselves.

background · pearl

Optical type

Inter with -0.022em tracking at display sizes mimics SF Pro's tight optical voice. Body text loosens so it reads quietly through the material.

font · inter / mono

Built for focus

Every interactive surface exposes a 3px system-blue focus ring that stacks on top of the bevel. Reduced-motion honored; hairline borders survive without backdrop filters.

a11y · WCAG AA+
